Brand Trust Baseline

How trustworthy does your brand look from outside? Run the Baseline self-test.

The self-test follows the CGBB Brand Trust Baseline: five sections and 15 core indicators from CGBB research and collaborative industry analysis. Answer the checks for your company, we add an automated scan of your website, and you receive an overall status, a 0–100 score, a five-section profile and your priorities by email. It takes about 20 minutes.

What the self-test gives you. As a CGBB member, your organisation has already been reviewed by the CGBB Secretariat, and once you confirm your website domain, CGBB confirms that the website is yours. The self-test adds your Baseline: an indicative status, score and priorities from your own answers and a scan of your website, so you can see where you stand and show that your company takes trust seriously.
