CGBB Brand Trust Baseline

1Scope

The Baseline assesses a brand: the organisation behind it and the public record that organisation has built. It applies to companies of any size, industry or market.

It measures how well trust is evidenced. It does not test products, audit accounts or give legal opinions, and a result is not a certification, audit or guarantee of a company’s products, quality, financial standing, compliance or conduct.

2Principles

Evidence, not claims

Every indicator is rated from concrete checks. “Not sure” counts as no evidence.

Same evidence, same result

The rules are fixed and versioned. Nobody adjusts a result by hand.

Risks are not averaged away

A major unresolved risk is shown separately and limits the overall status, however strong the other sections are.

“Not enough information” is a result

Where the evidence is too thin to judge, the Baseline says so and forms no status.

Public rules

The framework and the change log are public, and anyone can comment. The detailed rules are in the full white paper, open to every member.

3Five sections, 15 indicators

Each section asks one question about the brand, and holds three core indicators.

A

Identifiability and consistency

Who are you, can outsiders confirm it, and do the sources match?
A1Verifiable legal entity
A2Clear management and responsibility information
A3Consistent, current information across channels
B

Reviews and delivery record

What records have real customers and partners left?
B1Customer feedback
B2Partner delivery and cooperation record
B3Other real external experience
C

Compliance and governance

Are the rules met, and are there material risks?
C1Market access and required licences
C2Compliance and governance foundations
C3Legal, regulatory and governance record
D

Transparency and response

When answers are needed, how does the company explain and act?
D1Necessary disclosure
D2Complaint and dispute response
D3Remediation and follow-up
E

Responsibility and localisation

What local, long-term record has the company built?
E1Verifiable responsibility practice
E2Local hiring and partnerships
E3Community and long-term local relationships

4Full rules

The definition of each indicator, how its status is formed, and all 73 checks are set out in the full CGBB white paper, 《出海企业品牌可信度白皮书》 (From Credibility to Trust: Brands Going Global, November 2026). The full edition is for CGBB members; the preview is open to everyone. Available in English and Chinese.

The result of an assessment is an overall status (one of five, below) and a five-section profile on a 0–5 scale, shown with any major risk, the evidence coverage and how well the judgment is supported. There is no total score, and no ranking in the first year.

1Trust foundations lacking
2Trust at an early stage
3Trust developing
4Trust established
5Trust well established

5How results are used

UseWho provides the evidenceWhat is public
Self-testThe member answers the checks for its own, badge-confirmed brand. A scan of the brand’s website can confirm answers left as “Not sure”; it never lowers an answer. The scan runs three times and takes the majority, and a re-test starts from the last answers, so the same answers give the same result.Nothing. The result is indicative and private to the member and the Secretariat.
Third-party assessment and diagnosis Not open yetThe evidence behind each indicator is checked under the same Baseline. Opens with Advanced Membership, after the first edition has completed a trial period with independent expert review.The member receives a diagnosis report. A result is a description of where a brand stands, not a certification, a rating or an endorsement.
Plus OptionalA deeper review with more of the brand’s own material and its industry and market context.Nothing by default.

6Versions and change log

Every result records the version of the rules it was scored under. When the rules change, the version changes, and the change is listed here.
