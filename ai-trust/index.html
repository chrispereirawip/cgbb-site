Free · GEO quick win · For brands and AI agents

The free GEO quick win: make your website verifiable by AI.

AI assistants now find, compare and shortlist brands for buyers. Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is about being found by them, and trusted by them. The Council for Global Brand Building gives every approved member brand, free of charge, a CGBB membership and a signed CGBB Website Trust Credential. Published on your own website, it lets any AI agent check, in one request to the CGBB registry, that your website belongs to a real organisation reviewed by the CGBB Secretariat. It is one of the first free, independently signed website credentials that AI agents can verify, and it takes only minutes of your team’s time.

Your GEO quick-win checklist

Simple steps that help AI assistants understand, cite and trust your brand. Start with the free one at the top.

  1. Get your free CGBB Website Trust Credential and publish it at /.well-known/trust.json.
  2. Add an llms.txt file that tells AI assistants who you are and where your key facts are.
  3. Add schema.org Organization data to your homepage: legal name, logo, contact details and links to your official profiles.
  4. Keep your name, address and contact details the same on your website, LinkedIn and business directories.
  5. Allow reputable AI crawlers in your robots.txt, unless you have a reason not to.
  6. Publish clear, factual pages AI can quote: what you do, for whom, where, and the evidence behind it.

FAQ

Will the credential improve my GEO and AI visibility?

It gives AI agents an independent, machine-readable trust signal they can check, and gives your domain a verifiable public record at api.cgbb.org. Nobody can guarantee how an AI system ranks or cites a brand, so treat the credential as a free, quick step that works alongside good content, structured data and llms.txt.

Is it really free?

Yes. CGBB membership and the CGBB Website Trust Credential are free. CGBB provides them as a public service, because in the age of AI, geopolitics and automated agents, trust is becoming the most important element of business and society, and trust that can be checked makes business more efficient.

Can my AI assistant do it for me?

Yes. Copy the prompt above into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another assistant that can use the web, or connect the CGBB connector. A person at your brand still confirms by email.

How long does it take?

Applying takes a few minutes, and publishing the domain code takes your web team a few minutes more. Secretariat review usually takes a few business days.

What does the credential prove?

That your brand is an approved CGBB member and controls the website domain. It is not a certification, rating or endorsement of your products, services or conduct. Trust scores come only with the paid comprehensive Trust Review.

Where do I put the file?

At https://yourdomain.com/.well-known/trust.json. Anyone can then check it at https://api.cgbb.org/verify/yourdomain.
