Trust pathway

From diagnosis to certification.

Three steps, each more rigorous than the last, all built on the seven dimensions of the Global Brand Credibility Index.

01
Trust Diagnosis
Free · Self-serve

An automated first read on your brand’s credibility. Enter your company and answer a short questionnaire to receive an indicative score across the seven dimensions, with suggested next steps. Indicative only: it certifies nothing.

For members · Start your diagnosis →
02
Trust Assessment
Paid · Verified

An in-depth assessment based on evidence your company submits. Every analysis is checked by a CGBB reviewer before results are issued.

By application
03
Trust Certification
Panel certified

Certification signed off by the CGBB panel. Certified companies carry the CGBB badge, renew annually and become eligible for the 2027 ranking.

Renewed annually
